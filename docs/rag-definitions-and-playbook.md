# CLL-SPM Status RAG Definitions and Lead Playbook

One page. Everything a project lead needs to submit a monthly update.
Source: status-reporting spec, design D5/D6/F4-F5.

## What "status" means

| RAG | Meaning | You can say this when... |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | On track | The item is progressing to the current forecast. Nothing needed from leadership. |
| **Amber** | At risk, but recovery is within YOUR control | You see a threat, and you have a credible plan you can execute without help. State the plan in the summary. |
| **Red** | Off track, OR you need help beyond your authority | Something is broken that you cannot fix alone — budget, staffing, a decision, an external dependency. **A Red update requires a Path to Green or a Decision Ask. The system will reject it without one.** |

You also pick a RAG for **schedule**, **scope**, and **resources** separately; the overall
RAG is your judgment, not automatically the worst of the three.

## The monthly cycle

1. **Reminder** arrives before the period ends, with a link that already knows your item ID.
2. **Open the link** — the form is pre-filled with your item. No hunting for IDs.
3. **Fill in**: overall + schedule/scope/resource RAGs, a short progress summary,
   next milestone and its forecast date, percent complete, and — only if you need one —
   Decision Needed with a one-paragraph ask.
4. **Submit.** Target: **≤ 15 minutes**.

If you miss the due date, you get an overdue reminder. Five business days after that,
your unit head is notified. Nobody wants that email; submit something short and honest
instead of nothing.

## What each RAG obligates you to

- **Green** — nothing extra. A one-line summary is fine.
- **Amber** — say what the risk is and what you're doing about it, in the summary.
- **Red** — fill in **Path to Green** (what would fix it, roughly what it takes) **or**
  **Decision Ask** (what you need leadership to decide/provide, by when). Both is best.
  Red updates with neither are rejected by the form — that's by design, not punishment:
  leadership's first question about any Red is always one of those two answers.

## Decision asks

If you mark **Decision Needed**, your item appears at the **top of the Dean's overview**
and on the TCC governance view until the next update resolves it. Write the ask so a
decision-maker could act on it without calling you first: the decision, the options,
the deadline, and the cost of not deciding.

## Honesty > polish

- A stale Green is worse than a fresh Amber. Leadership trusts a dashboard that says
  Amber more than one that says Green for seven weeks and then Red.
- Percent complete is your estimate; trend matters more than precision.
- Status updates are append-only history — correcting a past update means writing a new
  one, never editing the old (same as the decision log).

## Quick reference

- Update cadence: monthly (biweekly only if the TCC sets it for your Tier 1 item).
- Your item ID looks like `CLL-26-0042`; the reminder link carries it.
- Questions: Strategic Operations (portfolio analyst).
