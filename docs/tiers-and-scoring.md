# CLL-SPM Registration Tiers and Scoring Rubric

Status: Proposed — TCC calibrates rubric weights on its first batch (design D3).
Source: design.md D3, portfolio-registry + intake-governance specs.

## 1. Tier criteria

A proposed work item is classified at triage by the criteria below. **Any one Tier 1
criterion qualifies the item as Tier 1 regardless of size.** If none apply and no
Tier 2 criterion applies, the work is operational and is **not registered**.

| Tier | Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Strategic** | Cross-unit work | Involves ≥ 2 CLL units (lead unit + contributing units; captured on the intake form, design D14) |
| | External commitment | External partner or client commitment |
| | Cost | Estimated cost ≥ $25,000 |
| | Effort | Estimated effort ≥ 400 staff hours |
| | Strategic deliverable | Named Strategy 2035 deliverable |
| **2 — Unit** | Effort | ≥ 80 staff hours |
| | Duration | ≥ 6 weeks |
| | New launch | A new program or service launch |
| **Operational** | — | Below all Tier 2 thresholds → not registered |

Compliance-mandated work (e.g., SEVIS/F-1 reporting changes) is registered by tier as
usual and categorized **Compliance**. It bypasses scoring ("must do") but still receives
a logged decision in the Decisions list.

### Decision rights

| Tier | Decides intake | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | TCC | TCC may set an individual Tier 1 item to biweekly status cadence |
| 2 | Lead unit's head | TCC has a 10-business-day call-up right after approval (design D11) |

## 2. Tier 1 scoring rubric

Scores **inform** the TCC's decision; they do not decide it. Each component is scored
1–5 per request; the weighted total is displayed side by side in the governance view.

| Component | Weight (proposed) | 1 = | 5 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic alignment | 25% | unrelated to any Strategy 2035 priority | named deliverable for a priority/objective |
| Expected value | 25% | marginal, unquantified | large, quantified benefit to learners/college |
| Urgency / compliance need | 15% | no time pressure | hard external deadline or compliance mandate |
| Capacity available | 15% | no staff available this quarter | staffed and ready to start |
| Effort (inverse) | 10% | very large effort | very small effort |
| Risk (inverse) | 10% | high delivery/execution risk | low risk |

Compliance-mandated requests are marked **"Must do"** and are not scored.

**Calibration:** the TCC adjusts weights on its first scored batch and this document is
updated to match; the rubric lives here, not in any flow or dashboard, so recalibration
is a doc change, not a system change.

## 3. Registration thresholds quick check

> Does the work involve 2+ CLL units, an external partner/client commitment, ≥ $25K cost,
> ≥ 400 staff hours, or a named Strategy 2035 deliverable?
> **→ Tier 1. TCC decides.**
>
> Otherwise: is it ≥ 80 staff hours, ≥ 6 weeks, or a new program/service launch?
> **→ Tier 2. Unit head decides; TCC may call up within 10 business days.**
>
> Otherwise **it is operational work — no registration needed.**
